Your presence at Riyadh Season

Riyadh Season is one of the Kingdom's largest entertainment and cultural events, with multiple zones and experiences drawing millions of visitors over several months. Presence here is different from a typical trade show — it calls for a full experience environment, not just a conventional booth.

From exhibition booth to experience environment

Riyadh Season opens the door to a wider range of presence formats than a conventional booth — interactive zones, institutional environments, and projection mapping that gives a brand a presence people actually remember.

From render to real experience

We design the environment or zone digitally first so every detail is approved, then execute it with the same engineering precision — whether it's a display pavilion, an interactive environment, or projection-mapping content.

Built to run for the long haul

Unlike short trade shows, Riyadh Season events run for weeks or months — which requires an engineering build that holds up to sustained, heavy use throughout the run without any drop in quality.
