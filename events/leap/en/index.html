Your booth at LEAP

LEAP is one of Saudi Arabia's and the region's flagship technology exhibitions, bringing together major global companies and government innovation programs each year under Vision 2030. A tech showcase at this scale needs a booth that matches the ambition.

What makes a LEAP booth different

LEAP visitors and exhibitors judge a brand from the first look at the booth, before any conversation happens. With hundreds of tech booths competing for attention, execution quality — not just size — is what actually stands out.

The Render-to-Reality method

We start with an accurate 3D render you approve before anything is built, then execute to the same engineering specs all the way to the final on-site result. No surprises between what you signed off on and what you see on opening day.

One contract, one team

Design and execution sit under a single point of accountability — no coordination gaps between separate contractors, no finger-pointing if a delivery slips or a detail changes.
