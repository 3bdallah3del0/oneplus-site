Exhibition Booth Brief Checklist: Step-by-Step Guide

To prepare a professional exhibition booth brief, define your core objectives, target audience, budget boundaries, and technical requirements. Use a detailed exhibition booth brief checklist to align your team and ensure your designer delivers an impactful concept.

Why You Need a Clear Exhibition Booth Brief

A well-structured brief prevents misunderstandings, keeps the project within scope, and accelerates the design phase. When working with exhibition stand builders in Saudi Arabia, clarity from day one ensures your brand identity translates accurately into a physical space.

Define Your Goals and Objectives

Before detailing design aesthetics, clarify what you want to achieve at the event in Saudi Arabia.

  • Generate qualified sales leads or direct sales
  • Launch a new product or service
  • Increase brand awareness within your industry
  • Network with key stakeholders and partners

Establish Your Budget and Timeline

Financial parameters and schedules are critical components of any brief. Be transparent about your investment ceiling to help your designer propose realistic materials and structures.

  • State the overall budget range without restrictive figures
  • Outline key milestones, including the design review phase which typically takes 3-7 business days
  • Account for execution and installation times, which generally range from 1 to 20 days depending on the project scale

Specify Space and Technical Requirements

Exhibition venues in Saudi Arabia have specific technical guidelines, height restrictions, and utility supply rules.

  • Provide the exact booth dimensions and floor plan layout (island, corner, or linear)
  • List required utilities such as electricity, water, compressed air, and hanging points
  • Mention storage needs for promotional materials, bags, and personal items

Outline Design Preferences and Branding

Guide the creative team on your visual identity while allowing room for professional design expertise.

  • Share brand guidelines including logos, fonts, and Pantone colors
  • Indicate desired zones such as meeting rooms, reception desks, and product display areas
  • Specify interactive elements like LED screens, touch-screen kiosks, or lighting effects

Frequently asked questions

What is an exhibition booth brief?

An exhibition booth brief is a document that outlines your company goals, budget, spatial requirements, and design preferences for trade show participation.

When should I send the brief to my stand builder?

You should send the brief several months before the event in Saudi Arabia to allow adequate time for design, approvals, and venue coordination.

How long does the exhibition stand design process take?

The initial design phase typically takes between 3 to 7 business days, depending on the complexity of the requirements.

How long does booth construction and installation take?

Execution and on-site installation generally range from 1 to 20 days, depending on the scale and structural complexity of the project.

Do exhibition venues in Saudi Arabia have height limits?

Yes, most exhibition centers in Saudi Arabia enforce strict height limits and rigging regulations that must be considered in your brief.
