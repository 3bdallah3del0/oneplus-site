LEAP exhibition booth guide: what does your booth need?

A successful LEAP exhibition booth requires high-impact spatial design, reliable power infrastructure for heavy tech demos, and seamless integration of interactive digital displays to engage visitors effectively within Saudi Arabia's premier tech event.

Understanding the LEAP Environment

LEAP attracts hundreds of thousands of technology professionals, innovators, and investors to Riyadh. To stand out in this high-energy environment, your exhibition stand design must balance bold visual branding with functional visitor flow.

Key Spatial Requirements

  • Dedicated reception areas for lead capture and initial greetings.
  • Open demonstration zones with secure hardware mounting.
  • Private meeting rooms for confidential stakeholder discussions.
  • Sufficient storage space for promotional materials and personal belongings.

Technology and Interactivity Integration

Tech companies exhibiting at LEAP need robust technical setups. Your build must account for elevated floor channels to hide cables, high-speed wired internet connections, and adequate electrical load capacities to support LED walls, touchscreens, and live product demos without tripping circuits.

Lighting and Visual Impact

Strategic lighting transforms a standard structure into an eye-catching landmark. Combine ambient overhead illumination with focused spotlighting on key products and backlit signage to draw foot traffic from across the hall.

Compliance and Exhibition Logistics in Saudi Arabia

Navigating local venue regulations is critical for a smooth build. Stand designs must comply with height restrictions, hanging point load limits, and fire safety codes mandated by Riyadh Front exhibition guidelines. Working with a local partner ensures adherence to municipal safety standards and streamlined venue approvals.

Timeline and Execution

Planning your LEAP exhibition booth requires a structured timeline to avoid last-minute stress. Initial concept design typically takes between 3 to 7 business days, while physical fabrication and on-site installation generally range from 1 to 20 days depending on the project scope and complexity.
